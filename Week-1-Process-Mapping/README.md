# Week 1 — Salesforce Process Mapping and Documentation

## Project overview

Document lead management, opportunity tracking, and case resolution as clear business workflows. Identify roles, inputs, activities, decisions, handoffs, outputs, and potential control points.

> **Project type:** Educational assignment and functional learning exercise  
> **Environment:** Training scenario; simulated materials only

## Planned deliverables

- Report (.docx) and PDF companion
- Current-state and proposed process maps
- Workflow descriptions and findings register
- Short presentation

## Scope and evidence boundary

Maps and findings are illustrative training artifacts. No real organization was audited, no live Salesforce org was accessed, and no time savings are claimed.

Any quantitative examples added here must identify their source or be explicitly labeled **simulated training data**. Proposed designs must not be described as deployed functionality. Cite public references for Salesforce product behavior and best practices, and exclude personal, customer, or proprietary information.
