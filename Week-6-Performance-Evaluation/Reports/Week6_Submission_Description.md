# Week 6 — Submission Description

This report demonstrates a simulated performance evaluation and continuous-improvement planning exercise for Salesforce operations in a fictional Cedarline Office Products environment. It defines five operational measures: lead assignment timeliness, case SLA attainment, record completeness, confirmed duplicate rate and training completion. For each measure, the report explains the calculation, intended interpretation and important denominator or data-quality considerations. A six-week synthetic dataset is supplied with numerator and denominator values so that the percentages can be reproduced and independently checked. The report includes example calculations for Week 6, a dashboard specification, proposed planning thresholds, source-report field requirements and a validation checklist.

The synthetic trend series shows lead assignment timeliness increasing from 70.0% to 91.8%, case SLA attainment increasing from 75.6% to 93.1%, record completeness increasing from 78.0% to 95.0%, confirmed duplicate rate decreasing from 9.0% to 3.0%, and training completion increasing from 68.0% to 100.0%. These values are provided solely to demonstrate KPI calculation and reporting. They are not actual Salesforce metrics, approved service targets or evidence of achieved business benefits. The report explains limitations including the absence of record-level lineage, intervention dates, control groups, seasonality and operational context, and it cautions against drawing causal conclusions from the illustrative trends.

A proposed dashboard layout includes KPI cards, weekly trend visuals, filters only where supported by source data, and an operational exception table. The improvement plan follows a Plan–Do–Check–Act sequence: establish a baseline, diagnose process causes, prioritize actions, pilot controlled changes, compare results using consistent definitions and maintain governance. The report also recommends assigning metric owners, documenting formula changes, reconciling source counts and obtaining approval before publishing KPIs. All data and scenarios are synthetic; no live Salesforce telemetry, connected dashboard or deployed improvement was accessed or implemented. This submission demonstrates analytical method and improvement planning, not live-system performance management.
