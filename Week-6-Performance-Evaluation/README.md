# Week 6 — Salesforce Performance Evaluation and Improvement

## Project overview

Show how simulated operational data can support a baseline KPI review, dashboard concept, and prioritized improvement plan.

> **Project type:** Educational assignment and functional learning exercise  
> **Environment:** Training scenario; simulated materials only

## Planned deliverables

- Clearly labeled synthetic dataset
- KPI definitions and formulas
- Dashboard mock-up and interpretation
- Recommendations and follow-up measurement plan
- Report (.docx)

## Scope and evidence boundary

All metrics and outcomes are simulated training data. They are not Salesforce telemetry, measured business results, or proof that an improvement was implemented.

Any quantitative examples added here must identify their source or be explicitly labeled **simulated training data**. Proposed designs must not be described as deployed functionality. Cite public references for Salesforce product behavior and best practices, and exclude personal, customer, or proprietary information.
