# Week 2 — Salesforce Data Quality Audit

## Project overview

Plan a structured audit of account, contact, and opportunity records using completeness, validity, duplication, consistency, and relationship checks.

> **Project type:** Educational assignment and functional learning exercise  
> **Environment:** Training scenario; simulated materials only

## Planned deliverables

- Synthetic sample dataset and data dictionary
- Audit checklist, scoring method, and findings summary
- Cleanup and prevention plan
- Report (.docx) and PDF companion

## Scope and evidence boundary

All records and audit results are simulated training data. No live Salesforce records were accessed or changed.

Any quantitative examples added here must identify their source or be explicitly labeled **simulated training data**. Proposed designs must not be described as deployed functionality. Cite public references for Salesforce product behavior and best practices, and exclude personal, customer, or proprietary information.
