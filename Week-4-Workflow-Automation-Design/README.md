# Week 4 — Salesforce Workflow Automation Design

## Project overview

Design a proposed Salesforce Flow approach for lead assignment and case routing, with entry conditions, decisions, exception paths, and operational controls.

> **Project type:** Educational assignment and functional learning exercise  
> **Environment:** Training scenario; simulated materials only

## Planned deliverables

- Automation logic and process diagrams
- Configuration notes
- Simulated test cases and fault-handling plan
- Deployment and rollback checklist
- Report (.docx)

## Scope and evidence boundary

This repository describes a design and paper walkthrough. No Flow, Process Builder, or Workflow Rule was activated in a Salesforce org.

Any quantitative examples added here must identify their source or be explicitly labeled **simulated training data**. Proposed designs must not be described as deployed functionality. Cite public references for Salesforce product behavior and best practices, and exclude personal, customer, or proprietary information.
