# Salesforce CRM Operations Learning Portfolio

A six-week portfolio of structured Salesforce Operations Associate learning assignments. This repository brings all six project guides together under one roof, with a separate folder for each week.

> **Training and simulation scope:** This portfolio is educational. Business scenarios, records, audit findings, process maps, configuration examples, diagrams, and KPI values are simulated or illustrative unless a source is clearly cited. It does not claim employment as a Salesforce administrator or consultant, access to a live Salesforce organization, production configuration, customer data, deployed integrations, executed automations, or realized business benefits.

## Six-week roadmap

| Week | Project | Focus | Project folder |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Process Mapping and Documentation | Lead, opportunity, and case workflows | [Open Week 1](Week-1-Process-Mapping/README.md) |
| 2 | Data Quality Audit | Account, contact, and opportunity data quality | [Open Week 2](Week-2-Data-Quality-Audit/README.md) |
| 3 | Integration Analysis and Planning | Conceptual Salesforce, ERP, and marketing data flows | [Open Week 3](Week-3-Integration-Analysis/README.md) |
| 4 | Workflow Automation Design | Proposed lead assignment and case routing | [Open Week 4](Week-4-Workflow-Automation-Design/README.md) |
| 5 | User Support and Training Guide | User enablement and troubleshooting | [Open Week 5](Week-5-User-Support-Training/README.md) |
| 6 | Performance Evaluation and Improvement | Simulated KPIs, dashboard concept, and action plan | [Open Week 6](Week-6-Performance-Evaluation/README.md) |

## How the weeks connect

The work moves from understanding processes (Week 1), to data quality (Week 2), integration planning (Week 3), automation design (Week 4), user enablement (Week 5), and performance review (Week 6). Any cross-week examples should use clearly labeled simulated training data and consistent assumptions.

## Deliverables

Each weekly folder describes the intended report and supporting materials. Add the Word report requested by the internship submission portal and any relevant diagrams, PDFs, datasets, checklists, or training aids to the matching folder as they are completed. The folder guides currently define scope and planned evidence; they are not a claim that all reports or artifacts are finished.

## Evidence and accuracy standard

- Label generated records, mock screens, sample metrics, and scenario outcomes as **simulated training data**.
- Distinguish a proposed design or paper walkthrough from work actually configured or tested in a Salesforce sandbox.
- Cite public references used for product behavior and best practices.
- Do not include personal, customer, or proprietary data.
- Do not present modeled outcomes as measured impact.
